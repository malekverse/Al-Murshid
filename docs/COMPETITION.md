# Competition: Ameen, and the Fajr alarm apps

2026-10-23. Studied for the v3 plan (SPEC 2, 12 and 13: Phases 7b, 7c and 10 to 14). This describes
their public store pages, screenshots and site, in our own words; nothing of theirs is copied.
Sources at the end.

## Ameen at a glance

- **Ameen: Quran, Athan, Prayer** (TAKLEEMA CO). Free, no ads, presented as a waqf.
- iPhone, iPad, Mac, Apple TV and Vision Pro, and Android. Arabic and English.
- 4.7 stars from 4.3K ratings (US store), #193 in Books. A 539 MB download.
- Ships often: about twenty releases since February 2025.
- Its store privacy label (the developer's declaration) lists identifiers and usage data used to
  track, and data used for third-party advertising and analytics. No accessibility features are
  declared.

## What makes it feel premium

- **One identity everywhere.** Deep green with a lime accent, an open-book mark beside a calligraphic
  wordmark, soft green store backgrounds with a faint geometric pattern, generous radii, a floating
  rounded tab bar. The store screenshots tell one story with a short headline on each.
- **Home.** A greeting for the time of day, a calendar "leaf" with the Hijri and Gregorian day, a
  one-tap morning adhkar button, the five prayers as a grid with the current one counting down,
  and a grid of tools the user arranges ("your daily apps").
- **Five tabs:** Home, Player, Mushaf, Adhkar, Services.
- **Mushaf.** Ornamental surah headers, a floating mini player, tafsir of the selected ayah, up to
  three tafsirs side by side, search by ayah or surah.
- **Adhkar.** One card at a time with "1/12" progress, a repeat counter, auto-advance, optional audio.
- **Services hub.** Greeting cards, prayer times, Qibla, Islamic occasions, calendar, monthly
  timetable, personal occasions on the Hijri calendar.
- **Widgets.** Many (nine new in September 2026): countdown, prayer grid, Jumu'ah, khatmah
  progress, date, daily dua and daily hadith, with color options.
- **Player.** Reciter portraits, favorites, offline downloads, live audio from Makkah and Madinah,
  Quran radio, short curated recitations in four styles.
- **Beyond:** AI search over Quran, hadith and adhkar; an AI fatwa search over two scholars'
  rulings; a link to a memorization teacher platform; Haramain and Hajj content; Eid cards.
- **Landing page.** A live "next prayer in …" pill at the top, a phone mockup in a lime circle with a
  floating date card, an embedded player with reciter choice and live radio, a dark green closing
  call to action, reveals on scroll.

## What its users complain about

- Page-turn animation glitches, and a mushaf that freezes at surah and juz dividers.
- Wrong Fajr times in parts of North America.
- An aggregator counts many neutral or negative mentions about bugs (treat with caution).

## The Fajr alarm apps

None of the all-in-one apps, Ameen included, has a wake-up challenge. Dedicated apps do:

- **Fajrly:** math in three levels, no snooze, no way out.
- **Fajr: Alarm:** math, shake, a memory card game, plus Quran and adhkar.
- **Subhi:** a prayer-mat scan, math, Face ID; about $30 a year.
- **A "missions" Fajr alarm:** walk, push-ups, math, typing adhkar, holding to renew one's intention.
- **General alarms** such as AlarmK: QR scan, math, squats.

Their gaps are our design: subscriptions; scans of a prayer mat (a camera check-in on worship,
which SPEC 13 cuts); adhkar typed as a chore or religion as a quiz; no safe way out in an
emergency; and no link to the rest of the day (the real Fajr time, the sky, "I prayed").

## Where we lead (say it on the landing page and in the store)

- Private by design: no ads, no trackers, no account needed; camera and microphone never leave the
  phone.
- Accurate: prayer times tested against reference values in 20+ cities, high latitudes and DST.
- The living sky; the Journey without guilt; night reflection behind a lock; four mushaf editions
  with tajweed; the recitation check on the device; French.
- The web: fast public pages for every city, surah and ayah.
- Fajr Rise.
- Small: large assets download on demand (Ameen is 539 MB).

## Where they lead today (close the gap)

1. **Reviewed content in the app:** adhkar, duas, a daily hadith. We have none yet. The blocker is
   a reviewer, not code (SPEC 12, content track).
2. **Identity:** a final brand, icon, store screenshots and a landing page. We still use a
   temporary icon (Phase 7c).
3. **Audio:** the player's feel, favorites, live audio (Phase 10).
4. **Widgets and platforms:** many widgets, iPad, Mac, TV (Phase 13).
5. **Tools:** greeting cards, occasions, monthly timetable (Phases 7c and 12).
