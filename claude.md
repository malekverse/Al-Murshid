# Al-Murshid: "The Spiritual Compass"
## Project Overview
An AI-driven, 1:1 cross-platform spiritual mentor application built with Expo React Native. 
Target Platforms: iOS and Android.
Language: Multilingual with Arabic as the primary language.
Design Aesthetic: Modern, Islamic, highly impressive, premium, "addictive" UX/UI.

---

## I. The Core Philosophy
Unlike traditional Islamic apps that act simply as utility clocks, Al-Murshid uses **Adaptive Learning Algorithms** to meet users at their current level of faith ($Iman$) and knowledge ($Ilm$).

### 1. Core Religious Utilities
- **Accurate Prayer Times:** Location-based alerts (Fajr, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib, Isha) with customizable Adhan.
- **Qibla Finder:** Digital/AR compass pointing to the Kaaba.
- **Hijri Calendar:** Lunar calendar tracking significant events (Ramadan, Eids).
- **Zakat Calculator:** Assets/gold/silver based calculation.

### 2. Comprehensive Quran Features
- **Scripts:** Uthmani, Indo-Pak, Mushaf al-Madina.
- **Audio & Recitations:** Libraries of world-famous Qaris with "Follow Reciter" highlighting.
- **Tafsir & Translations:** Multiple commentaries and 40+ languages.
- **Word-by-Word Analysis:** Grammar, root, and meaning breakdowns for students.

### 3. Lifestyle & Community Tools
- **Halal & Mosque Locators:** GPS maps for restaurants and masjids.
- **Dua & Azkar:** Categorized supplications with audio and counters.
- **Digital Tasbih:** Haptic counter for Dhikr.
- **Progress Trackers:** Gamified dashboards (prayer streaks, fasting, reading goals).
- **Content Streaming:** Islamic documentaries and shows.

---

## II. Key Innovative Features

### 1. The AI "Fatherly" Coach (Core Intelligence)
- **Onboarding "Mithaq":** Conversational onboarding ("What is your biggest struggle today?").
- **Behavioral Analytics:** Observes user behavior, tasks, and quizzes.
- **Non-Judgmental Correction (NLP):** Gentle interventions offering Hikmah instead of lecturing.
- **Dynamic Learning Path / Growth Tree:** Leveling system (branches representing Salah, Akhlaq, Knowledge) requiring basics (Taharah, Salah) before complex theology.

### 2. Smart Fajr & Sleep Optimization (Circadian Deen)
- **Intelligent Alarm:** Calculates exact Fajr time, wakes user up with "Smart Verses".
- **Sleep-to-Fajr Calculator:** Back-calculates sleep from required wake times, sends "Wind Down" notifications.
- **Sunnah-Sleep Integration:** Syncs with Apple Health/Google Fit context. Suggests Qailulah (midday naps) based on sleep deficits.
- **Contextual Adhkar:** Uses GPS/Sensors to suggest appropriate Duas (driving, walking, shopping).

### 3. Ultra-Cool "Deen-Widgets"
- **Current State Widget:** Spiritual energy progress bar.
- **Verse of the Hour Widget:** Tailored to user's knowledge level.
- **Quick-Dua Action:** Context/location-aware dynamic button.

### 4. Personalized "Faith Notifications"
- Contextual nudges based on recent activities (e.g. encouragement for prayer streaks, educational follow-ups on browsed topics).

### 5. Privacy & The "Safe Space" (Privacy-First Protocol)
- **Edge AI / Local Processing:** Behavioral analytics processed on-device (zero-knowledge encryption) for latency and privacy.
- **The "Father's Vault":** Private diary for reflection, offering Istighfar routines instead of raw tracking.
- **Muhasabah (Self-Reflection):** Weekly spiritual audits.

### 6. "Fajr-Chain" & Social Proof
- **The Wake-Up Call Squad:** Friends circle (3-5 members).
- **Biometric Wudu Verification:** Face-ID/ML checks for face dampness to dismiss alarms. Failure triggers real-time squad voice calls.
- **Proof of Salah:** 1-second photos of Masjid interiors to build a spiritual heat map.
- **Virtual Ummah:** Shared Khatm readings.

## III. The User Journey: From "The Seeker" to "The Muhsin"
| Stage | AI Interaction Style | Focus Areas |
|-------|----------------------|------------|
| **Level 1: The Seeker** | Gentle, encouraging, simplified | Basic Taharah, 5 Pillars, Pillars of Iman |
| **Level 2: The Believer** | Consistent, habit-building | Sunnah prayers, daily Adhkar, basic Arabic |
| **Level 3: The Knowledgeable** | Deep, philosophical, challenging | Tafsir depth, Fiqh of transactions, Seerah analysis |
| **Level 4: The Muhsin** | Refined, focused on "excellence" | Tahajjud, Dawah skills, community leadership |

---

## IV. The "Sabiqoon" Gamification System
1. **The "Sunnah Streak" & Squads:** Squad-based barakah multipliers. Nudge buttons to wake up sleeping members.
2. **The "Knowledge Duel":** 60-second fast-faith quizzes. Rewards: "Knowledge Coins" (Noor points) to unlock premium AI voice skins.
3. **The "Sadaqah Jariyah" Referral:** Referrals earn "Hassanat" points based on the invitee's activities, visualized as a spiritual impact map.
4. **Monthly "Muwakhat" Challenges:**
   - *The Fasting 3:* Fasting "White Days" with group chat features.
   - *The Quran Trek:* Collective completion of the Quran.
   - *The Midnight Guard:* Anonymous status lights for Tahajjud prayers.
5. **The "Deen-Leaderboard":** Avoids "Riya" (showing off). Displays titles (Al-Talib, Al-Muqeem) instead of raw levels. Ability to gift Adhkars (tokens).
6. **Referral Rewards / Sadaqah Pool:** Real-world charitable donations unlocked when referred friends reach Level 2.

## V. Technical Development Requirements
**Frameworks & Architecture:**
- Frontend: Expo & React Native.
- Language: TypeScript.
- State Management: Context / Redux / Zustand (TBD).
- Local ML & AI: Determine best libraries for Edge AI (on-device NLP sentiment and classification), biometric checks (Face verification/dampness logic).

**UI/UX Aesthetic Guidelines:**
- **Primary Vibe:** Modern, minimalist, invisible UI for widgets.
- **Style:** Islamic premium design (modern typography, subtle patterns - maybe geometric or architectural). Dark modes with vibrant/luxurious accents (Gold, Deep Emerald, Sapphire).
- **Animations:** Fluid, micro-animations for interactions to make the app feel "alive" and highly engaging.

---

## To Keep in Mind:
1. **Multilingual (Arabic First):** Ensure full RTL layout support across all screens and components from Day 1.
2. **Privacy as Priority:** Design data schemas so zero sensitive reflection data hits a centralized server.
3. **AI Responsiveness:** The AI must feel like a "Father" or "Mentor", necessitating fast on-device inference where possible.
4. **Battery Optimization:** Features like sleep tracking, location sensing for Adhkar, and local AI processing must be strictly optimized to prevent battery drain.
